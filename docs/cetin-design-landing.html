I design systems for software where mistakes are expensive. Coming from an industrial design and usability engineering background rather than visual art, I approach software as a precision instrument: built for transactional safety, cognitive ergonomics, and zero-debt scalability.

Right now, I lead Design Systems at Agito. I'm the sole designer shaping the platform architecture, token pipelines, and shared component standards for 14+ core insurance products and 250+ engineers.

My work bridges large-scale consumer ecosystems and dense back-office engines. Previously, I led product design across 5 agile squads for Allianz Türkiye's B2C ecosystem (1.5M MAU and $24M+ in written premium), architected multi-tenant commercial underwriting tools for Berkshire Hathaway’s Three Insurance at Radity in Zurich, and led payment discovery for eLogo across 20+ industries.

My foundation is an Industrial Design master’s degree from Yaşar University, where my thesis empirically evaluated interface friction, cognitive load, and trust dynamics in monetary transactions. That transactional mindset guides how I build: treating software not as a visual canvas, but as a fault-tolerant control system.
