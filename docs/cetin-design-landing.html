I design systems for software where mistakes are expensive. Rooted in usability research and human factors rather than visual art, I build precision instruments engineered for transactional safety, cognitive ergonomics, and zero-debt scalability.

Today, I lead Design Systems at Agito as the sole designer governing interaction patterns, component behaviors, and design system standards for 14+ core insurance products and 250+ engineers.

My work bridges large-scale consumer ecosystems and dense back-office engines. Previously, I led product design across 5 agile squads for Allianz Türkiye's B2C ecosystem (1.5M MAU and $24M+ in written premium), architected multi-tenant commercial underwriting engines for Berkshire Hathaway’s Three Insurance at Radity in Zurich, and directed payment discovery for eLogo across 20+ industries.

I hold a Master of Arts in Art & Design from Yaşar University, where my research drew on industrial design methods to evaluate interface friction, cognitive load, and trust dynamics in monetary transactions. That background defines how I build: treating software as a fault-tolerant control system.
