01 / Discovery

B2B Multi-Sector Discovery: The Cashflow Friction

eLogo (the fintech arm of Logo Yazılım, Turkey’s largest ERP software provider) identified critical collection inefficiencies across 20+ corporate verticals (construction, wholesale distribution, FMCG, automotive suppliers).

B2B buyers were trapped in manual bank transfers, unverified payment slips, and disconnected accounting ledgers.

02 / Benchmarking

14 Global & Local Fintech Benchmarks

I conducted an extensive competitive benchmark spanning 14 enterprise payment gateways (Stripe Connect, Adyen, Braintree, PayU, and Turkish bank virtual POS gateways).

Architectural Positioning

"B2B transactions require flexible payment term scheduling, partial settlement matching, and ERP ledger reconciliation—capabilities absent in standard B2C checkout flows."

03 / Information Architecture

Information Architecture & Gateway Flow

I architected the dual-sided platform structure: an administrative merchant dashboard for corporate sellers, and a friction-free payment portal for corporate debtors supporting single-link payments, recurring installments, and multi-currency billing.

04 / Impact

ERP Integration & Market Impact

The product blueprint established direct automated reconciliation with Logo Tiger and Logo Netsis ERP suites, transforming manual collection reconciliation from days to instantaneous automated ledger updates.
