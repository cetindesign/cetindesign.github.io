01 / Ecosystem

The 1.5M MAU InsurTech Giant: 5 Squads Orchestration

Allianz Türkiye is the leading private insurance and pension provider in Turkey. As UX Strategist and Product Designer partnering through Ting (Istanbul & London), I led experience strategy across 5 distinct product squads powering the "Allianz’ım" digital ecosystem:

  • Health Squad: Policy limits, contracted hospital networks, and digital claim reimbursement.
  • Private Pension (BES) Squad: Wealth management, government contribution tracking, and portfolio rebalancing.
  • Life & Auto Squad: Policy renewals, road assistance dispatch, and claims tracking.
  • Non-Life (Property/Casualty) Squad: Home and earthquake policy operations.
  • Value-Added Services Squad: Customer loyalty programs, wellness integrations, and preventive health benefits.
02 / Discovery

The Discovery Engine: 8,923-Respondent Research

To diagnose why pension policyholders exhibited high drop-off rates during digital fund rebalancing, I directed a large-scale quantitative and qualitative discovery initiative involving 8,923 survey respondents and in-depth user testing sessions.

Key Discovery Insight

"Users did not avoid fund distribution changes because of laziness; they avoided it out of financial anxiety and impenetrable actuarial jargon. Presenting fund returns without contextual risk bands caused immediate user abandonment."

03 / Omnichannel

Omnichannel Customer Journey: Connecting Digital, Bank & Telesales

Allianz pension products are distributed through three non-aligned channels: mobile application, bank branches (e.g. Yapı Kredi, Garanti), and direct telesales.

I mapped the Omnichannel Journey Blueprint to eliminate information asymmetry:

Channel Touchpoint Legacy Disconnect Unified Omnichannel Solution
Bank Branch Advisor initiates contract; customer receives paper contract with zero digital follow-up. Instant SMS push with real-time onboarding checklist in the Allianz’ım mobile app.
Mobile App Fund changes made on mobile were not synchronized with branch advisor CRM. Unified state synchronization with real-time advisor notification stream.
Telesales Customer service agents had no visibility into uncompleted mobile fund flows. Contextual drop-off tags passed to telesales for proactive assisted completion.
04 / Health Diagnostic

Health Coverage Diagnostic: 5.4M Queries & -20% Call Center Load

Health insurance users frequently flooded the call center before doctor visits to ask: "Is this hospital covered? How many outpatient limits do I have left?"

I redesigned the Health Limits & Contracted Network Architecture:

  • Dynamic Limit Progress Bars: Instant visual breakdown of remaining outpatient visits, inpatient coverage, and prescription limits.
  • Geo-Located Contracted Hospitals: Filter hospitals by specific insurance tier (Network A/B/C) with single-tap navigation and coverage guarantee badges.
  • Outcome: Scaled to 5.4 Million annual limit queries and contributed to an estimated 20% reduction in inbound health policy support calls.
05 / Wealth Flow

Fund Distribution & Wealth Management Flow

Private Pension (BES) users manage their lifelong savings in mutual funds. I redesigned the fund distribution interface:

  • Replaced complex financial tables with Risk Profile Sliders and automated robo-advisory suggestions.
  • Visualized government contribution matching (30% state subsidy) alongside personal accumulated capital.
  • Facilitated over 515,000+ completed digital fund distribution transactions.
