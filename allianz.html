Allianz Türkiye: High-Scale B2C Ecosystem

How cross-squad product design strategy, an 8,923-respondent discovery engine, and omnichannel journey architecture drove $24M+ in written premium impact and reduced call center load for Turkey’s largest private insurer.

1.5M+ Monthly Active Users
5.4M Health Limit Queries
515K+ Fund Transactions
$24M+ Written Premium

01. 5 Cross-Functional Squads Orchestration

Allianz Türkiye is the leading private insurance and pension provider in Turkey. Partnering through Ting (Istanbul & London), I served as UX Strategist and Product Designer leading experience architecture across 5 dedicated product squads powering the Allianz’ım digital ecosystem:

  • Health Squad: Policy coverage limits, contracted hospital network search, and mobile claims reimbursement.
  • Private Pension (BES) Squad: Wealth management, fund distribution, and government contribution tracking.
  • Life & Auto Squad: Policy renewals, on-demand roadside assistance dispatch, and claims tracking.
  • Non-Life Squad: Property, home, and compulsory earthquake policy operations.
  • Value-Added Services Squad: Customer loyalty programs, wellness integrations, and preventative care benefits.

02. The 8,923-Respondent Discovery Engine

To diagnose why pension policyholders exhibited high drop-off rates during digital fund rebalancing, I directed a comprehensive research initiative combining quantitative survey data from 8,923 respondents with deep qualitative usability sessions.

“Users did not avoid fund distribution changes because of disinterest; they avoided it out of financial anxiety and impenetrable actuarial jargon. Presenting raw fund tables without contextual risk bands caused immediate abandonment.”

03. Omnichannel Customer Journey: Mobile, Branch & Telesales

Allianz pension products are distributed through three non-aligned channels: mobile application, bank branch advisors (Yapı Kredi, Garanti), and telesales. I mapped an integrated journey blueprint to bridge channel drop-offs:

Touchpoint Legacy Channel Disconnect Unified Omnichannel Experience
Bank Branch Advisor initiates contract; customer receives static paper terms with zero digital follow-up. Instant SMS push with real-time onboarding checklist in the Allianz’ım mobile app.
Mobile App Fund changes made on mobile were invisible to the branch advisor CRM. Unified state synchronization with real-time advisor notification feed.
Telesales Support agents had no context on uncompleted mobile fund rebalancing flows. Contextual drop-off tags passed to telesales for proactive assisted completion.

04. Health Coverage Diagnostic: 5.4M Queries

Health policyholders frequently flooded the call center before medical visits asking: "Is this hospital covered? How many outpatient visits do I have left?"

I redesigned the Health Limits & Contracted Network Experience:

  • Dynamic Limit Progress Bars: Instant visual breakdown of remaining outpatient visits, inpatient coverage, and prescription quotas.
  • Geo-Located Contracted Hospitals: Single-tap filtering by specific insurance network tier (A, B, C) with coverage guarantee badges.
  • Measured Outcome: Scaled to 5.4 Million annual limit queries, directly contributing to an estimated 20% reduction in inbound support calls.

05. Fund Distribution & Wealth Management Flow

Private Pension (BES) users manage their lifelong savings in mutual funds. I restructured the rebalancing flow:

  • Replaced complex financial tables with Risk Profile Sliders and automated robo-advisory suggestions.
  • Visualized the 30% government contribution match alongside personal accumulated capital.
  • Facilitated over 515,000+ completed digital fund rebalancing transactions.

06. Measurable Business Outcomes & Scale

The redesigned ecosystem established a new benchmark for European insurtech:

  • $24M+ Written Premium Impact: Driven through in-app renewals, cross-sell campaigns, and streamlined pension top-ups.
  • Industry Recognition: Awarded MMA Smarties Global Gold and MMA Smarties EMEA Gold for mobile business impact.
  • User Satisfaction: 4.8★ App Store rating maintained across 1.5M+ active policyholders.
